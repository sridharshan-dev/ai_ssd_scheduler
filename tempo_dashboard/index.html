Where Host-Only Scheduling Collapses vs Where TEMPO Excels
Low Contention (0–50 IOPS): Flash channels are idle; host priority alone suffices (~3–5% misses).
Transition Zone (100–150 IOPS): Channel collisions begin. Host scheduler cannot see which die is busy.
Severe Contention (200–800 IOPS): S1 miss rate explodes to 29.2%–48.5%. TEMPO maintains 5.9%–16.2% by routing around busy dies!
